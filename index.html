Symptom rule: stop an exercise if it repeatedly drives pain, tingling or numbness farther down the leg or clearly worsens neurological symptoms. Seek urgent assessment for new bladder/bowel disturbance, saddle numbness, or rapidly progressive weakness.

Exercise photos: loaded from external physiotherapy/fitness sources, so an internet connection is required for the photos.

Gluteal strengthening: both sessions include three separate 1-minute seated hip-abduction exercises with a mini resistance band: upright, leaning forward and leaning backward, with a 10-second rest between each position. This is followed by 1 minute of crab walking with the band around the knees. The four supplied demonstration images are embedded directly in the app.
